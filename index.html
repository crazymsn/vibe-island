FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Vibe Island really free?

Yes. Vibe Island is free and open source. There is no paid tier, no trial timer and no account to create. You can read the source on GitHub.

Which AI coding agents are supported?

Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, OpenCode, Droid, Kiro and dozens more. Integration depth varies per tool, and you can add your own with the beta custom agent support.

Which terminals can it jump back to?

iTerm2, Terminal.app, Ghostty, Warp, WezTerm, Kitty, Alacritty, Zed, Zellij, tmux and the integrated terminals of VS Code, Cursor and Windsurf. Some of them support exact tab, split and tmux pane targeting; others activate the app on a best-effort basis.

Does my data leave my Mac?

Vibe Island is designed to run locally with no cloud account required. Check the source and the repository for exactly what it does on your machine.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 14 or later. Macs with a notch show the island inside it; other Macs and external displays get a floating bar at the top center of the screen.

Can I monitor agents on a remote server?

Yes. SSH Remote lets you watch agents running on remote machines from your Mac, with reconnect and multiple servers.

How do I contribute?

Open an issue, suggest an integration, improve the docs, test another terminal, or send a pull request.
